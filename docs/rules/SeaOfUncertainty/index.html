Sea of Uncertainty

An operational naval wargame of command, information, tempo, and combat — built to preserve the visceral pleasure of maneuver and combat while making uncertainty, timing, friction, and command genuinely matter.

You are not trying to eliminate uncertainty. You are trying to operate better than your opponent inside it.

Design North Star

High Player Agency

Phases exist to present choices, not merely process bookkeeping.

Approachable Detail

Detail stays only when it changes what the player might decide.

Risk & Reward

Strong actions expose costs: time, information, signature, endurance, command attention, or weapon depletion.

Bounded Luck

Players construct the odds. Dice reflect the uncertainty remaining after decisions have shaped the situation.

Move Things

Task forces, submarines, aircraft, and support assets still maneuver across a real operational map.

Pew Pew Pew

Detection, targeting, missile salvos, reaction, air defense, torpedoes, damage, and force preservation remain tangible.

Source Foundation

The underlying theoretical inspiration is the CNA / Naval War College study Transforming Naval Wargaming: A Framework for Operational-Level Wargaming (originally published 2004; reprinted 2024). The study frames operational wargaming through six dimensions — time, space, forces, effects, information, and command — and connects them through operational, informational, and command topologies. It also emphasizes friction, uncertainty, entropy, and timely synchronization as core problems of operational command.

Useful source touchpoints in the uploaded PDF: pp. 1–5 for the overall framework; pp. 15–29 for friction, entropy, command, and uncertainty; later sections for topology and timing; Appendix A for the Road to Baghdad example.
