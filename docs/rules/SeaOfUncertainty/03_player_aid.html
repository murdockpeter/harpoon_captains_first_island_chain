What Choices Do I Have?

A phase-by-phase decision guide. The point is not to memorize procedure — it is to see the choices, understand the tradeoffs, and make the system reflect your plan.

When the Clock Reaches a New Time

1

Situation Update

What do I actually know right now?

Trust the current pictureReward: preserve tempoRisk: act on stale / false information
Seek better informationReward: improve targeting and confidenceRisk: time, exposure, command attention
2

Command

Where does my attention matter most?

Let Missions continueReward: preserve HQ capacityRisk: local situation may have changed
Change a MissionReward: adapt deliberatelyRisk: command load and mission churn
Plan / synchronizeReward: stronger combined actionRisk: delay and coordination friction
3

Ready Formations

Continue, intervene, or recover?

Continue current MissionReward: speed, simplicity, delegationRisk: less precise control
InterveneReward: direct adaptationRisk: consumes attention
RecoverReward: restore readiness/cohesionRisk: surrender tempo
4

Movement & Search

Where must I be, how fast must I get there, and how visible can I afford to be?

Cautious MoveReward: concealment / lower frictionRisk: slower position
Normal MoveReward: balancedRisk: no special advantage
High TempoReward: arrive soonerRisk: signature / endurance / friction
Passive SearchReward: concealmentRisk: weak detection
Active SearchReward: stronger detectionRisk: reveals presence
Focused SearchReward: best local intelligenceRisk: consumes Command Attention
5

Engagement

Do I fire now, wait, synchronize, or disengage?

Strike NowReward: seize tempoRisk: weaker information / coordination
Wait for Better TargetingReward: better attack qualityRisk: target moves / enemy acts first
SynchronizeReward: compress enemy reactionRisk: delay / stale intel / friction
Heavy Weapon CommitmentReward: higher penetrationRisk: depleted future striking power
Light CommitmentReward: conserve weaponsRisk: insufficient effect
6

Friction & Next Action

Do I accept the schedule reality, or spend command effort forcing the plan back on track?

Accept DelayReward: preserve HQ capacityRisk: lose synchronization / tempo
Push ThroughReward: preserve timingRisk: Command Strain
Pause to RecoverReward: regain effectivenessRisk: opponent gains initiative

Before Every Major Strike

  1. Is the target worth revealing myself for?
  2. Is my information good enough right now?
  3. What happens if I wait?
  4. What happens if I attack immediately?
  5. Can another force contribute in time?
  6. How much offensive capacity am I willing to expend?
  7. What will I have left if this is the wrong target?
  8. What reaction am I allowing the defender?
The player builds the situation. The dice only tell us exactly how reality expressed the uncertainty that remained.
