Sequence of Play & Decision Aid

Use this beside the board. It compresses the expanded rules without hiding the important choices.

10-Step Sequence

  1. Advance Time.
  2. Update Information.
  3. Select Ready Formation.
  4. Choose One Action.
  5. Choose Posture / Commitment.
  6. Enemy Reacts.
  7. Resolve.
  8. Apply Consequences.
  9. Check Entropy.
  10. Schedule Next Action.

Search

Targeting

Low −2 • Medium −1 • High 0 • Identified +1.

Strike

Light +0 • Standard +1 • Heavy +2 + Weapon Expenditure.

Reaction

Defend +1 Defense • Evade +1 Defense + move 1 after • Counterattack Light strike if capable • Hold no modifier.

Synchronization

1 Slot; declare Strike Time. First attack normal Defense; second −1; third+ −2. Three+ participants mark Friction.

Entropy

Friction = +1 Time after complex Actions. Disruption = −1 Search/Sync. Destruction = −1 offense. 0 Low; 1 Moderate; 2 High/Disrupted; 3 Critical/Disorganized.

Command

3 Slots. Focused Search • Immediate Retask • Synchronization • Push Through • Restore Command • Force complex Action.
