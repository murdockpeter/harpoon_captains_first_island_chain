# Scenario 5 authoritative specification

## Ghost Fleet

Source: *Harpoon: First Island Chain* p. 25 and dummy card p. 22, with dummy-task-force and sonar rules from *Captain's Rules* p. 9.

### Forces and setup

Scenario 5 replays Scenario 4's complete order of battle, deployment limits, detection rules, destination, and victory conditions. The US Navy receives three dummy cards and PLAN receives five. Each allotment begins in a side-private dummy group at its side's setup position and may be redistributed into friendly real or newly created dummy task forces.

### Dummy task forces

- A task force containing only dummy cards cannot search, detect, or attack.
- Dummy cards may move between friendly task forces at will. A transfer is publicly verified as dummy-only, including across hexes, but neither formation's unrelated contents are exposed.
- Empty dummy groups are removed; their undrawn movement chit is removed with them. A newly created pregame dummy group receives a movement chit.
- Dummy totals are conserved. When sonar clears a dummy group, its cards return to another friendly real task force for later reuse.

### Detection and hidden information

A successful SSR or visual search of a dummy-only task force reports **no surface ships present** and records its position, but does not classify it as an attackable surface formation. The contact remains consistent with either a dummy or a submarine. A successful sonar search removes the dummy task-force counter.

Side-private snapshots expose a player's own dummy counts but redact all enemy dummy counts. Verified transfers are public, while private logs, rules transactions, unit contents, and undetected positions remain sealed. The distinct 3D contact buoy represents an unresolved force without falsely drawing a ship model.

### Playability ruling

Captain's Rules impose no movement limit on dummy task forces but warn that implausible movement reveals the deception to a human opponent. The current controls expose the normal plausible declared-speed range so players can operate the deception without an accidental UI tell; dummy transfers and new task-force creation remain available throughout play.
