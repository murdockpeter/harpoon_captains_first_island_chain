# Scenario 8 authoritative specification

## Hunt the Dragon

Source: *Harpoon: First Island Chain* p. 26, modern platform cards on pp. 15–16 and 18, and the submarine and damage rules from *Captain's Rules*.

### Forces and task forces

PLAN deploys four independently activated surface task forces:

- Fujian (Type 003), the objective carrier;
- one Type 055 cruiser;
- one Type 052D/DL destroyer;
- one Type 054B frigate.

The US deploys four independently activated submarine task forces: two Virginia-class SSNs and two Los Angeles-class SSNs.

### Entry and patrol axis

- PLAN starts on the printed `0812`–`1212` line.
- PLAN formations must remain within two hexes of the row-12 westbound patrol axis. Treating the printed segment as a westbound axis preserves both its two-hex formation restriction and its explicit west-edge objective.
- US submarines begin off map. On activation, their first movement point enters any navigable hex on column 15, the east edge. Off-map submarines cannot be detected or attacked.
- The western exit is the westernmost PLAN-navigable water hex in a row. This follows the playable sea boundary where the printed map's physical west border is land.

### Carrier air-group capability

The modern carrier card represents one complete embarked air group as one scenario-scale capacity unit. Fujian begins at `1/1`. Scenario 8 does not launch individual flights; it tracks the air group because launch capability is part of the victory condition.

Fujian can launch aircraft only while it has an embarked air group, is afloat, and has less than half damage. For its six-box hull, the third hull hit crosses the half-damage threshold and causes a launch mission kill. This state is included in saved games and multiplayer snapshots.

### Victory

- The US wins immediately when Fujian sinks.
- PLAN wins immediately when Fujian spends a movement point to leave the western edge while still capable of launching aircraft.
- A launch-incapable Fujian cannot claim the victory exit.
- The game lasts seven complete turns. If neither printed victory condition occurs by then, the result is a draw.

### Presentation

The east entry edge is cyan, the PLAN patrol band is navy, and the west escape boundary is orange. Fujian uses a larger carrier silhouette with a flight deck, island, runway stripe, and deck aircraft. Its formation card shows air-group capacity and launch status.
