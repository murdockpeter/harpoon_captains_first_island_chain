# Scenario 7 authoritative specification

## Lifeline to Taiwan

Source: *Harpoon: First Island Chain* p. 25, modern platform cards on pp. 15, 19, and 21, and submarine rules from *Captain's Rules* pp. 10–11.

### Forces and task forces

US Navy:

- two Constellation frigates;
- one Arleigh Burke Flight IIA;
- three merchant ships;
- one tanker.

PLAN:

- two Type 039A/B (Yuan) SSKs;
- one Type 093B SSN.

The default deployment uses four independently activated US groups—three escorted merchant convoys and one tanker group—and three independently activated PLAN submarine task forces. Every non-arrived, non-destroyed formation receives its own movement chit. Pregame splitting can create additional legal surface or submarine formations.

### Deployment

- US formations deploy in navigable water within two hexes of `0910`.
- PLAN formations deploy in navigable water anywhere except Taipei / Zuoying (`0810`) and the area within two hexes of `0910`.
- A formation cannot begin in the destination port. This makes the printed requirement to **reach** Taipei meaningful and prevents setup from satisfying the objective before play begins.
- In solo play, PLAN positions are selected deterministically from legal concealed hexes using the match seed. The US player may adjust each friendly starting hex before the first chit draw. Hot-seat and network sides may place their own formations.

The board uses a subdued teal tint for the US assembly area, purple legal-placement highlighting, and the existing green destination port.

### Port entry and arrival

When an active US formation enters `0810` with a surviving merchant, it is marked arrived, expends its remaining movement, leaves the operational map, and receives no future movement chit. Arrived ships remain in the roster and score display but cannot be detected, targeted, or attacked.

The tanker does not count as one of the three merchant ships and entering the port with only the tanker does not satisfy an arrival.

### Victory

The game lasts ten complete turns. The US qualifying total is:

`surviving merchants that arrived + offsets for merchant losses = 3`

Each sunk PLAN submarine supplies at most one offset, and an offset applies only to an actually sunk merchant. Thus, two arrived merchants plus one lost merchant plus one sunk PLAN submarine is a US victory. A surviving merchant that fails to reach Taipei does not count and creates no loss to offset. If the qualifying total remains below three after turn ten, PLAN wins.

The game may end early once the US qualifying total reaches three. Destroying every PLAN submarine does not prematurely stop convoy movement when the arrival requirement has not yet been met.
