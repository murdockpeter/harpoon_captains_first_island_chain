# Scenario 2 authoritative specification

## Flagship Duel

Source: *Harpoon: First Island Chain* p. 25, with platform cards on pp. 15–16 and 18 and the unchanged surface-combat procedure from *Captain's Rules* pp. 3–7.

### Order of battle

- PLAN: one Type 055 (Renhai), at hex `0510`.
- US Navy: two Arleigh Burke Flight IIA destroyers, one Ticonderoga cruiser, and one San Antonio LPD, at hex `1213`.
- Detection is omitted, as it is for the first two introductory surface-combat scenarios.
- No turn limit is printed.

### Victory

Every marked hull hit on every opposing warship counts. The side with the larger total wins; equal totals are a draw. Sinking one ship does not end the scenario while the opposing formations can still change the total. Force destruction, a mathematically fixed score, disengagement, concession, or mutual scoring can end play.

### Formation-scale decisions

The US player can form two independent defensive pairs. Pairing is chosen during each legal defensive deployment and may be changed for a later engagement. Missile factors can be divided into independent salvos against multiple target ships. Long-range removals and short-range defensive assignments remain explicit, so rollback pressure is resolved per salvo and pair rather than by collapsing the formation into one defense value.

### Printed setup discrepancy

The scenario calls hex `1213` “three hexes southeast” of hex `0510`, but those explicit coordinates are ten hexes apart under the same axial topology confirmed by Scenario 1. The project follows the printed hex references because they are unambiguous setup instructions and records the contradictory prose here rather than silently substituting a coordinate.
