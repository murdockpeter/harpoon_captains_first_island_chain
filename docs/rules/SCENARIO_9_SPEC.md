# Scenario 9 authoritative specification

## Patroller

Source: *Harpoon: First Island Chain* p. 26, the P-8A card and roster on pp. 17 and 23, and patrol-aircraft rules on pp. 12–13 of *Captain's Rules*.

### Forces and setup

- PLAN controls three Type 039A/B SSK task forces and one Type 093B SSN task force, each deployed within ten hexes of Xiamen.
- The US controls one Los Angeles-class SSN deployed at least fifteen hexes from Xiamen.
- One P-8A Poseidon patrol-aircraft model is based at Kadena (`0904`) and is not freely redeployed during setup.

### P-8A model

The single model represents the scenario's serviceable aircraft of that type. Its modern card is ASR 1, SSR 3, ASM 0–2, sonar 4, ASW 5, radius 20, and defense 0. Four roster boxes track serviceable aircraft.

When its movement chit is drawn, the model may relocate directly to any map hex within twenty hexes of Kadena. It can use ASR, SSR, sonar, ESM, and visual search from its final station. Patrol ESM reaches three hexes. Visual search succeeds on 1–5 and is unavailable at night. The model may make one ASM or ASW attack per turn.

Radiating enemy ASR automatically detects an aircraft in range and can direct long-range SAM fire; a same-hex detected aircraft may also face the best short- or long-range SAM factor. Each SAM hit rolls the Aircraft Damage table: 1 has no effect, 2–3 aborts, and 4–6 shoots down an aircraft. Aborted aircraft return to base; shot-down results reduce the serviceable pool. A surviving ready aircraft can return on its later chit.

### Victory and presentation

PLAN wins immediately when its third submarine exits through the orange eastern edge. If fewer than three submarines escape by the end of Turn 15, the US wins. The board uses cyan aviation-range highlights, a procedural P-8A silhouette and patrol ring, an aviation-specific activation panel, and aircraft serviceability cards.
