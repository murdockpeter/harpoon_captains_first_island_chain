# Scenario 4 authoritative specification

## Picket Line

Source: *Harpoon: First Island Chain* p. 25, modern platform cards on pp. 15, 18, and 21, and detection rules from *Captain's Rules* pp. 8–9.

### Forces and setup

- US Navy: two merchant ships, one Arleigh Burke Flight IIA, and two Constellation frigates at Subic Bay (`0716`).
- PLAN: two Type 054A frigates and one Type 052D/DL destroyer.
- A human PLAN player may deploy the picket in any PLAN-navigable water more than four hexes from both Subic Bay and Taipei / Zuoying. Solo play uses a concealed deterministic placement derived from the match seed.
- Surface detection is mandatory. Undetected opposing positions and contents are hidden.
- No turn limit is printed.

### Victory ruling

The narrative requires the convoy to reach Taipei / Zuoying, while the printed victory sentence says the US must sink a PLAN “amphibious/escort force.” No PLAN amphibious ship appears in the listed forces. Reading that copied phrase literally would make both the convoy and destination irrelevant and refer to a nonexistent unit.

The project therefore uses this minimum coherent ruling:

- US victory when at least one merchant reaches Taipei / Zuoying (`0810`) or the complete listed PLAN picket is destroyed.
- PLAN victory when both merchants are sunk before either arrives.
- Disengaging or mutually scoring before either US condition is met awards PLAN the printed “otherwise” result.

### Hidden information

The host remains authoritative. Until classification, a side snapshot omits the opposing position, unit IDs and state, movement path, private contacts, opposing commands, logs, and transaction trace. On classification the current position and formation contents become available. The full trace is released after game end.

### Arrival

Arrival is recorded when a US task force enters Taipei / Zuoying with at least one merchant afloat. Splitting is legal before the first chit, so an independently moving convoy element can satisfy the objective. The destination hex is highlighted green in the 3D map.
